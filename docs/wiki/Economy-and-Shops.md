# Economy and shops

## Money

You start with $500,000. There is no income: this is all you have to prepare with. The money shows in the top bar.

## Plots

You can own one base plot. Buy it at Hillside Realty (travel there first).

| Plot | Size | Price | Water |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old Farmstead | 40 x 30 m | $120,000 | A working old well (3 litres an hour) |
| Warehouse Yard | 30 x 26 m | $180,000 | None |
| Creekside Lot | 20 x 20 m | $150,000 | Stream |
| Deep Woods Clearing | 26 x 24 m | $95,000 | Stream |

The grid is 2 m per cell.

## More land

Click your plot and choose **Buy more land**. Each purchase adds a 10 m strip along one edge, up to four strips per side.

Price = strip area x (plot price per square metre) x 0.85 x (1 + 0.2 x strips already bought on that side).

A side is blocked by a road, a building, the dirt road, a neighbouring plot or the edge of the map. Trees on the new land
are cleared for you, and the woods just outside are thinned all round so you can see your base from any angle. Structures
never move when you buy land.

The names of the edges ("upper right" and so on) follow the way you have turned the view.

## Shops

| Store | Sells |
| --- | --- |
| Supermarket | Food, water, household goods |
| Hardware | Materials, tools, some ready-made fences, generators, panels, tanks |
| Pharmacy | Medical supplies (10% dearer) |
| Gun shop | Weapons, ammunition, armour |
| Farm supply | Seeds, soil, feed, farm structures |
| Gas station | Fuel (15% dearer) |

**SurvivalMart Online** sells everything the shops do plus the whole range of structures, from anywhere, with instant
free delivery, for a 6% convenience markup. Orders go straight into storage.

Each shop has limited stock that comes back over the days (20% of normal each day).

## Panic buying

From Day 20 the world starts to panic. Prices rise and stock falls, more for food, water, fuel, medicine and weapons
than for building materials. By Day 30 the most affected goods can have lost 75% of their stock. Buy early.

## Returns

Unused items in storage can be sent back to the online store from the storage list (**1**, **All** or **Return**) for 80%
of the shelf price. Panic prices never count, so a return can never make a profit. Fresh produce cannot be returned, only
items the store sells can, and returns close at the outbreak. Placed structures use **Take down**, which puts them back in
storage whole (worn ones give back scrap, see [Upkeep and repairs](Upkeep-and-Repairs.md)).

## Orders and news

`O` lists recent orders. `N` is the news log: radio and TV, and messages about your base. Warnings make a red toast and
drop the game speed back to 1x.

## Product sheets

Click any product in a store to see what it is, what it does, how it works and the numbers. The sheets use the same
tables the simulation uses. Anything the game does not use yet says so.
