# Z-Game wiki

Z-Game is a single-player zombie-apocalypse base builder that runs in the browser. You have 30 days to get ready. On
Day 30 the shops close and the outbreak begins.

- [Controls](Controls.md): every key and mouse action

- [The world and walking](World-and-Walking.md): the big map, chunks, walking, waypoints
- [Vehicles](Vehicles.md): the seven vehicles, driving, fuel, repairs and parts
- [Looting and carrying](Looting-and-Carrying.md): searching houses after the outbreak, weight, the backpack
- [Economy and shops](Economy-and-Shops.md): money, stores, panic buying, returns, land
- [Building and placing](Building-and-Placing.md): placing from storage, the structure card, moving and rotating
- [Survival and utilities](Survival-and-Utilities.md): food, water, power and how they connect
- [Weather and seasons](Weather-and-Seasons.md): the four seasons, rain, snow, storms, and what they do to you
- [Clothing and character](Clothing-and-Character.md): the character creator, warmth, dryness, the eighteen clothes

## Keeping these pages up to date

The pages in `docs/wiki` are the source. To publish them on the GitHub wiki, copy the files into the wiki repository
(`Z-game.wiki.git`). `_Sidebar.md` is the wiki menu. When you change how the game works, change the matching page in the
same commit.
