# Base Architect

Press `P`, or press **Plan base** in the advisor. The Architect looks at the base you have, tells you what is missing,
designs an upgrade around what is already standing, tests the design, and builds it for you when you say so.

It only **adds**. Your existing fences, wells, farms and cabins stay where they are.

## What it checks

Beds, food, water, power, how much of the outer wall is closed, watchtowers and floodlights, seed reserves and upkeep
(worn and dirty structures, and whether you have the tools). Each line is green, amber or red.

## What it designs

The design is sized for **eight people**, twice the family you start with.

- **Outer wall** on the boundary of your land, in the strongest wall your money allows (concrete, then cinder block,
  log palisade, chain-link), with a steel **gate** on the side that faces the nearest road. If something is in the way
  it moves the gate along the wall.
- **Crop belt** just inside the wall: three orchard plots (no seeds needed), a large greenhouse, scarecrows. More
  greenhouses or beds are added if the belt cannot hold the usual set.
- **Inner yard** behind a second fence with its own gate lined up with the first. In it: two more **log cabins**, bunk
  beds until there are beds for everyone, a medical station, a fish pond, wells, tanks, a water tower, solar arrays and
  batteries.
- **Security**: four corner watchtowers, four floodlights, two sandbag gun nests and four spike traps by the gate, and a
  stone path from the gate to the yard.
- **Links**: pipe junctions and power poles wherever a farm or machine would otherwise not be connected.
- **Supplies**: seeds for about 40 days, a week of food and water, and an upkeep kit (the four tools and spare materials).

## Small plots

If the plot is too small for a crop belt around an inner yard, the Architect first plans the cheapest **strips of land** on
open sides until the plot is about 44 x 36 m, and buys them before ordering. It never buys across a road, a building or a
neighbour. If it still cannot fit something it says so in the notes, for example "no room for a fish pond".

If the neighbours and roads leave no land to buy, it falls back to a compact design without the inner fence, with the
crops around the edge and everything else in the middle.

## It tests the design

The Architect copies your game, builds the whole design in the copy, and runs four game days. It checks that food and
water cover eight people and that nothing is left unlinked or unpowered, and shows the result ("Tested: it runs by itself
for 8 people"). If power is short it adds panels and batteries and tests again.

## Cost and building

The panel lists the cost by group (land, water, food, power, housing, security, upkeep, paths) and how long your crew
needs. Buying uses the online store at current prices, so panic prices apply late in the game. If you are short, the
**Sandbox funds** button adds $500,000. **Build the upgrade** buys everything and lays it all out as plans; your crew then
builds it. If you are not at your base, the button takes you there first.

Plan again after building: it should find little or nothing left to add.
