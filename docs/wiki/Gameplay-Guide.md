# Gameplay guide

## The goal

Survive the outbreak on Day 30 with your family. Everything you do before then is preparation. After Day 30 the shops
close, nothing can be ordered or returned, and only what you built and stored counts.

The world clock runs faster than real time (default: a day in an hour). You are a person on a map 1.3 km across: you walk,
run or drive to the shops and to your land (see [The world and walking](World-and-Walking.md) and [Vehicles](Vehicles.md)),
and you build by placing structures from storage while you stand at your base.

## A sensible order

1. **Get a plot.** Pick the one that suits you. Old Farmstead is the biggest and has a working old well. Deep Woods
   Clearing is the cheapest and hidden from the road, but small. See [Economy and shops](Economy-and-Shops.md).
2. **Water first.** A person needs 3 litres a day and dies of thirst in about a day and a half. Build a well and a tank
   next to each other. A hand-pump well needs no power.
3. **Food next.** Build farms and buy seeds. Orchards and fish ponds need no seeds at all, so they keep working after the
   shops close. Keep a pantry of canned food while the crops grow.
4. **Power.** Solar panels and a battery run lamps, floodlights, pumps and electric fences. See
   [Survival and utilities](Survival-and-Utilities.md).
5. **Shelter.** Cabins, houses, tents and bunkers.
6. **Walls and watch posts.** A boundary wall with a gate, watchtowers, floodlights, gun nests and spike traps.
7. **Tools and spares.** Things wear out. Keep a toolbox, shovel, power tools and welding kit, and materials for repairs.
   See [Upkeep and repairs](Upkeep-and-Repairs.md).
8. **Buy early.** From Day 20 prices rise and shelves empty.
9. **Get wheels and fuel.** After the outbreak a vehicle with a full tank is how you reach the far houses. Buy it, a spare
   tank or two of fuel in cans, and spare parts while the shops are open.

## The panels

- **Your family** (left): health, thirst and hunger, then water, food and power at a glance.
- **Advisor** (left): the next step, urgent alerts, and a checklist. Its buttons buy and place things for you.
- **Plan base** (in the advisor): opens the [Base Architect](Base-Architect.md).
- **Dock** (bottom left): Build, Online store, Storage, Orders, News.
- **Structure card** (right): click anything you placed.
- **Warning signs** float over structures with a problem: a bolt for power, a drop for water, a fuel pump, a sprout for
  seeds, wheat for animal feed, a wrench for worn out, drops for dirty. Hover for the full text.

## Time and speed

See [Getting started](Getting-Started.md#game-speed). Walking and driving are in real time, so a long trip across the map at
4.6 m/s is a few game hours at the normal clock. Construction is done by a crew of three over game time. Every structure has a
build time. Searching a house takes 6 to 15 game minutes.

## After the outbreak
The shops close and the houses empty. Every house can be searched once for food, water, medicine, tools, fuel and spare parts: see
[Looting and carrying](Looting-and-Carrying.md). Carry what you find home, or load it into a vehicle.

## Losing

If thirst or hunger stays empty the family loses health, and at zero health the game ends. The last warning message says
why.

## Hints

- The product sheet (click a product in a store) explains what each item does and the numbers behind it.
- Fresh food from your farms goes into storage as baskets and is eaten first.
- A tank only fills if a well or collector is joined to it. A pipe junction reaches 14 m.
- Walls hold zombies off, but the zombies are not in the game yet. The defence structures are ready for them.
